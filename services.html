Colour & Colour Correction

Colour Is What We Love

Whether you want a subtle refresh or a bold change, we'll talk through what suits your hair and what's realistic, then get to work.

  • Hair colour: a shade chosen with you, for your hair and your style.
  • Toning: fine-tune your shade and keep your colour looking fresh.
  • Colour correction: colour that came out brassy, patchy or just not right? We'll assess it honestly and plan the fix.
